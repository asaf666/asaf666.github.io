01

Profile

// who_is_this.md

I'm a Senior QA Engineer with 20+ years in software quality, about ten of them in one of the least forgiving corners of software: cybersecurity. My security work spans identity protection, deception (honeypots), VPN/PKI, firewall management, and data loss prevention — across Linux and Windows endpoints, containers, cloud, and enterprise networking.

I've led automation engineers as a Tech Lead and I treat quality as an engineering discipline, not a gate at the end. My differentiator is domain depth: I understand the products I test as a security practitioner would, which means I find the failures that matter, not just the ones that are easy to reach.

In my own time I build automation and security tooling for the pleasure of it — a personal trading agent, network and firewall utilities, LLM-driven assistants. The instinct to instrument, monitor, and verify doesn't switch off.

Smart Trading Agent

Paper trading quant research · Python

A disciplined, rules-based research agent for US equities that scans, scores, and paper-trades while narrating its reasoning in plain English. A professional exit engine — break-even, partial scale-out, Chandelier trailing stop — sits behind a risk manager that acts as the sole hard gate, so capital preservation always comes first. Across a 5-year full-cycle backtest it beats SPY on a risk-adjusted basis (Sharpe 1.05 vs 0.86) with roughly 40% less drawdown, learns from its own realized trades, and can layer in walk-forward-validated ML signal weights and an LLM news analyst.

Financial Compass

Live stock research · Streamlit

A multi-market stock-research dashboard for US and Israeli (TASE) equities, built in Streamlit. It pulls live prices and fundamentals, computes a multi-factor composite score per stock, and layers on a news, sentiment, and macro-risk overlay drawn from both English and Hebrew sources — with full Hebrew display for Israeli names, interactive candlestick charts, optional Gemini AI analysis, and a disk-cached two-stage pre-screen so a ~200-name universe loads fast. Built for research and idea-filtering, explicitly not financial advice.
